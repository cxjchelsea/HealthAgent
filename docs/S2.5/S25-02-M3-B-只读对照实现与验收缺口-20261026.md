# S25-02 M3-B — offline parity guard (2026-10-26)

Status: **implementation started, read-only synthetic contract only**.
Baseline: M3-A commit `befc450d76553a8445b75f8f5d88cbda6257c147`.

The new `evals/s25_02_m3_readonly_parity.py` compares an M3
`PreTurnFacts` candidate with an explicit legacy observation without invoking
the API, tools, or persistence. The comparator distinguishes COMPATIBLE,
MISMATCH and INSUFFICIENT, but *never* grants authority. It detects:
- semantic route conflicts even when the old chain performed effects;
- legacy effects with missing new admission proofs;
- missing/unknown provenance of legacy observations;
- read-only clarification vs unexpected side effects.

**Important limits**:
- Frozen fixtures are regression controls, not evidence of real same-turn
  parity, and a caller-provided `observed_legacy` label does not prove its
  authenticity.
- Matching top-level route is not enough to prove exact tool arguments,
  checkpoint CAS, idempotency, or target/scope equivalence. More detailed
  comparison still required.
- Post-turn `facts_from_legacy_observation` must NEVER feed M3's pre-turn
  admission contract. M3-B must consume authenticated pre-turn snapshots
  and independently captured final legacy observations from the same
  conversation, request, and Turn identity.
- M3-C remains NOT AUTHORIZED; no current production dispatch wired.

**Next requirements**:
1. read-only same-turn facts capture with immutable request/turn identity;
2. strict target/scope/effect and actual side-effect comparison;
3. replay, concurrency, forged pending and stale plan tests;
4. real Qwen/API physical E2E under a disabled-by-default gate;
5. independent M3 cutover authorization.
